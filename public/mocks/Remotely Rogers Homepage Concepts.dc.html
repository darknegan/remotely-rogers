A note from Jeff

We bought this ridge because it was quiet, and we built the cabins far enough apart to keep it that way.

I grew up twenty minutes from here, so I know what the weekends look like in Northwest Arkansas: the trails fill up, downtown fills up, and then you want somewhere to go that doesn't. That's the whole idea.

If you text the number on the contact page, you get me. Happy to tell you which cabin suits your group, or which trailhead to hit first.
