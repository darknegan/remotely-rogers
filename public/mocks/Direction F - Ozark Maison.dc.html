Six cabins · Rogers, Arkansas

The A-frames at Remotely Rogers

Seventy wooded acres above Rogers, fifteen minutes from downtown and twenty from the Bentonville trails. Six private cabins, each with its own porch, and a host who lives up the road.

Check availability The cabins

An inn without a lobby: six houses in the trees, run like a small hotel.

We keep the cabins stocked the way we would want to arrive: firewood split, coffee ground, beds made in real linen. Each A-frame is fully private, so the only thing you share is the hill.

Come as two, as a family, as a crew of twelve. Bring the dog. Work from the deck on Thursday and drive into Bentonville on Friday.
